Connect or reconnect to QuickBooks

QuickBooks Payment Matcher is a private Windows desktop utility, not a browser-hosted application.

To authorize or reconnect, use the QuickBooks connection procedure in the installed desktop application. Follow the Intuit authorization screen and select the correct QuickBooks Online company.

This informational page does not initiate OAuth authorization, collect passwords, or store access tokens. The desktop application must implement the actual authorization and reconnection process.
