QuickBooks Payment Matcher

This is the information page for a private Windows-based QuickBooks Online payment reconciliation utility.

To use the utility, open the desktop application on the authorized Windows computer. This page does not process payments or receive QuickBooks account credentials.

Payment creation or changes to accounting data occur only through the separately installed desktop application and its authorized QuickBooks Online connection.
