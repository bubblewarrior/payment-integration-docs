Disconnect from QuickBooks

QuickBooks Payment Matcher is a private Windows desktop utility.

To end the app's QuickBooks Online access, use a disconnect/revoke option if offered by the desktop utility, or manage connected applications in your QuickBooks Online account.

This page is informational only. Visiting it does not revoke tokens, disconnect a company, or change QuickBooks data. Revocation must be carried out through an authorized app or QuickBooks account procedure.
