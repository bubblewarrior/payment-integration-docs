Payment Reconciliation Utility

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Payment Reconciliation Utility (the “Utility”), an internal-use tool that interacts with QuickBooks Online, handles information. The “Operator” is the organization or individual who deploys the Utility and authorizes its use for internal accounting workflows. The Utility is not offered to the general public.

1. Information involved

When an authorized person uses the Utility, information processed may include customer and invoice names or identifiers, invoice balances, payment amounts, remittance references and dates, payment-account selections, QuickBooks company identifiers, and information supplied in remittance spreadsheets or entered manually. Configuration details, operational status, exception reports, and audit records may also be generated.

2. Why the information is used

The Utility uses information to compare remittances against QuickBooks Online invoices, identify missing or mismatched records, display proposed allocations and exceptions for review, and, when an authorized user initiates the action, submit payment entries to QuickBooks Online. Information is not used for advertising or sold to data brokers.

3. Connections and disclosures

The Utility communicates with Intuit's QuickBooks Online services as necessary for authorized accounting tasks. Intuit processes data according to its own applicable terms and policies. The Operator may permit designated staff and support personnel to access the Utility or its outputs as needed for internal accounting and maintenance. Other disclosures may occur where legally required.

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Viewing this website does not connect visitors to QuickBooks Online or display accounting records. GitHub may collect website-access information in accordance with its own policies.

4. Storage and retention

Depending on the installation and configuration, the Utility or its operating environment may store application settings, connection tokens, working files, exported reports, and audit information. The Operator is responsible for deciding how long to retain those materials and for securing and deleting them under its internal record-retention practices. Accounting entries already submitted to QuickBooks Online remain governed by the connected QuickBooks account's controls and retention practices.

5. Security

Access should be limited to authorized personnel, and devices, credentials, and working files should be protected with appropriate security measures. No technology can guarantee absolute protection. QuickBooks access can be revoked by an authorized administrator using the applicable Intuit account or app-connection controls.

6. Data requests and questions

Authorized users should direct questions, requests to review or delete locally retained information, or concerns about the Utility to the application administrator through the internal contact channel used to provide access to the Utility. Requests concerning information held in QuickBooks Online may need to be handled through the QuickBooks account administrator.

7. Updates

This policy may be updated as the Utility or its data handling changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
